Here are some amazing science facts just for you!

🦋

Butterfly Taste

A butterfly uses its feet to taste food! It has taste sensors on its legs.

☀️

The Mighty Sun

The Sun is so enormous that one million Earths could fit inside it!

🦴

Baby Bones

Babies are born with about 270 bones, but adults have only 206 because some fuse together as we grow.

🐙

Three Hearts!

An octopus has THREE hearts! Two pump blood to the gills, and one pumps it to the rest of the body.

🌱

Oxygen Tree

A single large tree produces enough oxygen for two people to breathe every single day!

💧

Your Body is Water!

About 60% of your body is made up of water! That is why drinking water every day is so important.
